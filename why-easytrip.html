Why EasyTrip

One connected way to explore a travel idea

EasyTrip brings seasonal inspiration, sample stays and journey-planning prototypes into one clear frontend, built for a university programming project.

See how the pages connect

Ideas, stays and transport in one place

Trip planning often starts in several places at once: one site for ideas, another for somewhere to stay, another for getting there. EasyTrip puts those first decisions side by side, in one website connected by the same menu.

Every page shown here is part of the current site. Stays and prices are sample content.

Why the experience works

Connected exploration
One menu and footer link every section, and pages point to related ones. A season leads to places to stay, and stays lead to transport planning.
Clear choices
Each page narrows one decision: a season, a hotel or a chalet, a flight or a bus or train. Comparisons and filters help you choose without reading everything.
Responsive and accessible foundations
The interface is built to adapt from phone to desktop and to work with a keyboard, zoomed text, screen readers and reduced-motion settings.
Honest prototype behavior
Sample prices, planners and the contact form say what they are. They check what you enter and show a summary, but never book, charge or send anything.

How the pages connect

Follow one trip idea through four real pages, from choosing a season to reviewing a plan.

  1. Discover a season

    Compare winter, spring, summer and autumn ideas and pick the season that suits your trip.

    Explore destinations

  2. Explore sample stays

    Browse sample hotels and chalets, filter by price or setting, and open the details of a stay.

    Browse stays

  3. Plan how to travel

    Compare flights with buses and trains and pick the planner that fits your route. Both planners are prototypes.

    Choose a journey planner

  4. Understand the result, Prototype

    Enter a route, dates and travelers to get a checked summary. Nothing is searched live or booked.

    Open the flight planner

What works today, and what a backend could add

Everything in the first list works in the current site. The second list describes possible next steps for the programming project, not promises.

Available now

In the current frontend

  • Seasonal destination guides and travel content
  • Sample hotel and chalet collections
  • Search, filters, sorting and form checks in the browser
  • Summaries from the flight and bus and train planners
  • Responsive navigation and layouts

Future work

Possible with backend work

  • Destinations, stays and routes managed in a database
  • User accounts and sign-in
  • Saved itineraries
  • Reservation records
  • Admin tools for managing content
  • Contact messages processed on a server
